Templates

Home is a catalog of photo templates: Bring to Life, Dance, Sport, Birthday, and more. A generation can keep running while you browse. Premium templates are marked PRO.

Create

Write a prompt or tap Surprise me. Add a photo for image-to-video. Choose Veylumi 2.6, or with Premium Veylumi 3.0 and 3.0 Fast, plus 5 or 10 seconds, 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9, and Standard or HD.

Library

Ready videos stay in your Library. Filter by Ready, Processing, or Failed. Play a result, save it to the gallery, share it, or delete it.

Credits

A new account starts with 25 credits. A daily reward grows across a seven-day streak, then starts over. A generation spends credits. If it fails, or you cancel it while it is still running, those credits come back.

Premium

Weekly and yearly plans are billed by Google Play. Premium unlocks Veylumi 3.0, Veylumi 3.0 Fast, 10-second videos, HD, and premium templates. The price is shown in the app before you subscribe.
